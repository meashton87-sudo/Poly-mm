Two-Minute Drill is a free football game. No account and no email are needed. Here is everything the website keeps and who sees it.

On your device

Your game progress, your stats, your survivor run and the name you choose (if any) are saved in your browser's local storage, so a reload picks up where you left off. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

Daily results and leaderboards

When you finish the daily drive, your score is sent to our server on Cloudflare so you can see how everyone did. The same goes for your survivor best, if you put a name on the board. What we keep: the score, the date, the name you chose (only if you entered one) and a scrambled form of a random code your browser made up (so the same phone counts once a day and nobody else can take your name). No IP addresses, emails or real names are stored.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which parts of the game people use (for example, how many finish the daily drive), so we can make it better. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address and device details. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Ads

The site may show ads from Google AdSense on screens between games. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads in Google's Ad Settings, or visit aboutads.info to opt out of other vendors' personalized ads. Visitors in the EU, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Children

The game is meant for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If what the site collects changes, this page will say so.
