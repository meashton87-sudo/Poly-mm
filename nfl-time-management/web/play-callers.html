At the end of a drive the game tells you whose play-calling yours resembled: how often you ran, how fast you went, how much you gambled, matched against the real tendencies of the people who called plays in the NFL. This is the list it draws from: who really called each side, team by team and week by week, with the source for each. Every team and both sides from 2022 on; the defenses from 2016; the best-known offensive play-callers from 2006.

A head coach who called his own side is listed as its caller. Where the reporting is shared or unconfirmed, the entry is marked unconfirmed and left out of the matching. A caller is matched only with enough real snaps to make a fingerprint; the comparison is a likeness, not a judgment of the coach.
